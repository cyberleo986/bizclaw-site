Frequently Asked Questions

How long does setup take?

Discovery to live deployment typically takes 5-7 business days. Complex integrations may take 2 weeks. Rush deployment available for +$500.

What channels does the agent support?

WhatsApp Business API (official), SMS, email, voice calls, and web chat. We can also integrate with Telegram, Slack, Discord, and custom webhooks.

Where does the agent run?

We host on secure cloud infrastructure (your choice of AWS, GCP, or Azure region). For Enterprise, we can deploy on your private cloud or on-premise.

What happens when the agent can't handle something?

The agent is trained to recognize its limits and escalate gracefully. It passes full conversation context to a human, including customer intent, data looked up, and suggested next steps.

Can I see what the agent is doing?

Yes. Every conversation is logged with full reasoning. You get a real-time dashboard showing active conversations, escalation rates, customer sentiment, and agent performance metrics.

What's included in the monthly retainer?

Hosting, monitoring, model updates, security patches, conversation volume, quarterly business reviews, and continuous optimization based on conversation data.
