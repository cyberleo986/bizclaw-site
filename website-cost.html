What Affects Website Cost?

  • Pages: More pages = more cost. Most small businesses need 3-5 pages.
  • Design: Custom design costs 3-5x more than templates.
  • Features: Contact forms, booking systems, e-commerce add cost.
  • Content: Writing your own copy saves $500-2,000.
  • SEO: Basic SEO should be included. Advanced SEO is extra.

Hidden Costs to Watch For
