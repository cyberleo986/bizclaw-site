Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No coding required. Just find and replace the {{PLACEHOLDERS}} with your business info. If you can use Find & Replace in a text editor, you can launch this site.

What do I need to customize it?

Any text editor (Notepad, TextEdit, VS Code). Replace placeholders like {{BUSINESS_NAME}}, {{PHONE}}, {{EMAIL}}, {{CITY}} with your actual info.

Where can I host the website?

Anywhere. We recommend GitHub Pages (free), Netlify Drop (free), or any web host. The README includes step-by-step hosting setup.

Is the contact form included?

Yes. The contact page has a working form. Just sign up for a free Formspree account and paste your form ID. Instructions included.

Can I change colors and fonts?

Yes. Colors are controlled by CSS variables in one file. Change a few hex codes and the whole site updates. Full instructions in the README.

What's the refund policy?

7-day money-back guarantee. Not satisfied? Email us for a full refund, no questions asked.
