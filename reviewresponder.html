Common questions

Will it sound like a robot wrote it?

No. The AI is trained on thousands of high-quality responses from real businesses. It uses the actual review content, your brand voice (which we learn from your past responses), and adapts to sentiment. Customers can't tell.

What if I don't like a response?

Edit it before publishing. Most users approve AI drafts as-is 87% of the time. You can also set "auto-publish for 4+ star reviews" and only manually review 1-3 star ones.

Do I need to give you access to my accounts?

You connect your accounts via secure OAuth. We only read new reviews and post responses you approve. You can revoke access anytime.

What if I cancel?

You keep all your data. No questions asked. We stop posting responses but your existing reviews stay on Google/Yelp/wherever.

What about platforms like TripAdvisor or Booking.com?

Pro and Agency plans include TripAdvisor, Booking.com, Airbnb, and Apartments.com. Starter covers Google and Facebook.
